Privacy & community guidelines

Track The Track is an independent school project for recording running results and sharing workout ideas. It is not an official school record system or a medical service.

What your account stores

Supabase provides authentication and database storage. Your account includes your email address, display name, race results and notes, published workouts, likes, saved favorites, comments, and any reports you submit. Passwords are sent to Supabase for authentication and are not included in your account exports.

What other athletes can see

Your race results, notes, email address, and saved favorites are private to your account through the website. Signed-in athletes can see published workouts, author display names, likes, and comments. A display name is not a verified identity. Avoid posting your full name, school, location, contact information, or other information you want to keep private.

Content reports are visible to their author and site moderators. The site owner can administer the database, including private records, to operate and support the service.

Storage on your device

The site stores your login session in browser storage so you stay signed in. “Remember my email” optionally stores your email on that device. Password recovery uses temporary session storage. Sign out on shared devices and uncheck “Remember my email” if you do not want your address saved there. The site does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

Your choices

Use Account settings to change your display name, request a password reset, download your account data, or delete your account. You can also edit or delete individual race results, remove your comments and workouts, and undo likes or saves.

Account deletion removes your active account data. Deleting a published workout also removes comments, likes, and saves attached to it, including those from other athletes. Copies may remain in provider backups until they expire under the provider’s retention settings. Downloaded copies and information someone has already seen cannot be recalled.

Keep the community useful and respectful

Share relevant running sessions and constructive experiences. Do not post harassment, spam, someone else’s private information, or dangerous advice. Use “Report this workout” or “Report comment” to flag content for review. Moderators may remove inappropriate content. Reports are reviewed by a person; this is not an emergency reporting channel.

Posting is limited to 10 workouts, 60 comments, and 20 reports per account per clock hour to reduce spam. Each athlete can submit one report per item.

Workout ideas and the demo

Workouts are general session ideas, not personalized training plans. Adjust them to your experience and how you feel. Stop if you feel pain or unwell. The demo dashboard uses fictional results. Demo changes stay in the page’s memory and disappear on reload; they are never sent to the database.

Questions or bugs

For technical problems, open a GitHub issue. Issues are public: describe the problem without passwords, email addresses, private results, or other personal information. Use the in-app reporting controls for community content.
